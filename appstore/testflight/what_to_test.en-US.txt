Browse places on the Map tab (editorial places from OpenStreetMap), open a place card, sign in with Apple, add your own place, check in with a catch and a photo, record a trip (+ → Start a trip), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New:
• The Places tab: search, sections (nearby, popular, editors’ picks) and saved places — the bookmark on a place card.
• The map shows relief and contour lines — zoom into the mountains near Almaty.
• Offline maps: Tips → Getting ready — download an area and turn on airplane mode.
• Directions on a place card — 2GIS, Yandex or Google.
• Follow this route on a trip page — a warning when you leave the track.
• Check-ins and trips upload in the background: check in offline, leave the app — they are sent once the network is back.
