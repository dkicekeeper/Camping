Dalada

Fishing and the outdoors in the Almaty region and Zhetysu — an iPhone app.

  • Places on the map: lakes and rivers, fishing spots, campsites, springs, paid ponds.
  • Fresh reports: how the fish bite, water and road conditions, catches with photos.
  • Trip recording with a GPS track — works offline too.
  • Fishing rules and seasonal bans right on the map.
  • Friends, a feed, reviews and discussions of places. Your own spots can stay secret.
